      Every fruit and vegetable mapped across the calendar year so you can see exactly when it peaks. Southern-hemisphere timing, general across temperate regions — your local greengrocer's dates may run a few weeks either side.

      Note on timing. Australia spans tropical, temperate and arid climates, so a crop's window can shift by several weeks between, say, Queensland and Tasmania. A handful of items (avocado, mushroom, banana) run close to year-round — treat this as a general shopping compass, not a fixed timetable, and your local market as the final word. Icons are original illustrations, not photographs.

      Source. Month ranges are cross-checked against Sydney Markets' What's In Season guides, supplemented with general knowledge for varieties and niche items outside their published tables. Treat it as a well-informed guide rather than a precise forecast — growing conditions shift a crop's actual window from year to year. Picking tips reflect general, widely-known produce knowledge rather than a single external source.
